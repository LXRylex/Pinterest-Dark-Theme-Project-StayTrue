Info
How Private Pins Vault works and why it exists.
What is Private Pins Vault?
Private Pins Vault is a local saving feature. Instead of saving a pin to your Pinterest account, the extension stores the pin information inside your browser storage and shows it inside the Vault page.
1. You press Vault
The extension reads the current pin image, title, URL, and media link.
2. It saves locally
The pin is saved in this browser's extension storage, not to Pinterest boards.
3. You open Vault
Your saved pins appear inside the Private Pins page, separate from Pinterest.
4. You stay cleaner
Your Pinterest profile, boards, and saved history do not need to hold every reference you want to keep.
5. Edit with tags
Vault lets you edit saved pins with tags, organize them, and keep your references easier to search later.
6. Download your Vault
You can download all saved pins from your Vault when needed, so your local collection is not locked inside the page.
Why use it?
Keeps references away from your public Pinterest boards.
Useful for private inspiration collecting.
Less direct interaction with Pinterest saving.
Fast access from the popup or settings page.
You can tag, edit, and organize saved pins.
You can download all saved Vault pins when needed.
If the Vault button does not appear OR The Themes are not applied, hard refresh Pinterest with CTRL + F5, May need to press a few times.
